Train pool: v3 judged 39 train pairs, accuracy 88.5% (vs SMJ 92.5%, vs MgmtSci 84.2%). Reflection saw 5 misjudged, 4 low-confidence and 3 anchor pairs.

  - *why*: The board over-rewarded audience-accuracy framing and missed the Organization Science paper on firms interpreting market feedback to choose positions.
- **EDITED**: Posits countervailing effects, paradoxes or trade-offs, such as a benefit at one stage or condition and a penalty at another, and specifies contingencies.
  - *now*: Posits countervailing effects, trade-offs or self-limiting advantages with contingencies, framed as resolving tensions between theories or competing mechanisms.
  - *why*: Merged with the overlapping tension-framing rule; self-limiting advantages (strength at generating options, weakness at selecting) marked the Organization Science paper in the group-learning pair.
